Answers to Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about partnering with Mobile Lab.

Do we own the source code and infrastructure? expand_more

Yes, 100%. Unlike SaaS vendors or proprietary agencies that lock you in, every line of custom software, script, schema, and API integration belongs entirely to your organization upon project completion. We transfer full Git ownership and deployment scripts.

How do you prevent AI hallucinations in client-facing tools? expand_more

We never use unchecked conversational models. We implement strict Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) pipelines, deterministic schema enforcement, validation boundary layers, and human escalation fallbacks. If an answer cannot be verified against company documentation, the system gracefully routes the lead to a human.

What happens if our existing team already has developers? expand_more

We frequently work alongside in-house tech leads and CTOs. In those engagements, we operate as a force multiplier—taking on the heavy lifting of diagnostic workflow audits, specialized AI agent architectures, and background automation pipelines while your developers stay focused on your core product roadmap.

How quickly can we see working software? expand_more

Following the diagnostic phase, our typical sprint produces a demonstrable, working prototype within 10 to 14 days. We don't believe in multi-month dark periods where you wait blindly for software; you see and test interactive code early and continuously.
