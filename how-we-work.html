The Method in Action: VitalSpark SaaS

How our 4-step sequence took a health company from manual spreadsheet coaching to automated AI personalization in 4 weeks.

WEEK 1 · DIAGNOSE

The Bottleneck

VitalSpark trainers spent 4.5 hours per client manually writing custom workout splits across 7 different spreadsheets, limiting client intake to 15 members monthly.

WEEK 2 · DESIGN

The Architecture

Designed a deterministic prompt routing engine tied to an exercise biomechanics database with strict injury-prevention validation rules.

WEEKS 3-4 · PROVE

The Prototype

Shipped a working web app to 25 beta testers. Generated full 12-week workout and nutrition plans in 8 seconds, reviewed and approved by head coaches.

WEEK 4+ · REUSE

Full Handover

VitalSpark onboarded 150+ paid subscribers in month one with zero trainer burnout. Internal team trained to manage database and prompt models independently.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions regarding our timeline, process, and deliverables.

How much time does my leadership team need to invest? expand_more

We respect your calendar. During Phase 01 (Diagnose), we require roughly 2 to 3 hours total from key operators to review workflows and map handoffs. During development, you participate in weekly 30-minute review demos. We handle all architecture, coding, testing, and deployment.

Can we keep our existing tools (CRMs, databases, email providers)? expand_more

Absolutely. Our methodology emphasizes making your existing software work harmoniously rather than forcing a painful migration. We connect directly into HubSpot, Salesforce, Postgres, Google Workspace, Stripe, and specialized internal tools via clean API adapters.

What happens if the system requires maintenance later? expand_more

In Phase 04 (Reuse), we provide video runbooks and documentation so your in-house team can maintain the system. If you prefer continuous peace of mind, we offer our Managed Systems Retainer which provides 24/7 telemetry monitoring, drift prevention, and dedicated monthly iteration sprints.

What if the prototype reveals our bottleneck was different than expected? expand_more

That is precisely why we build working prototypes in days rather than waiting months. Our iterative process surfaces real operational truths early, allowing agile adjustments without wasting capital or burning team morale.
